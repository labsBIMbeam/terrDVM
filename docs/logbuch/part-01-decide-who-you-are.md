# ⚓ Logbuch der SEC — Part 1: Decide Who You Are

> *Written in salt, signed by claw. Every entry content-addressed, so no
> landlubber can rewrite the past.*

---

**Day 1. Somewhere off the coast of everything.**

They asked me at the harbour office for me papers. I gave them a hash.

`f352bcdc4f24dec97b7fbf40d0c50b9d8a69fa6947961a409a36e03601a8db42`

That's me. All of me. Not a name granted by a registry, not a licence
stamped by a port authority — bytes, and the digest of bytes. Verify me or
walk the plank; either way I don't change.

A pirate's first duty is the only one that matters: **decide who you are.**
Before the flag, before the ship, before the first city crumbles under
your feet. The ones who skip that step end up sailing someone else's
course, flying someone else's colours, holdin' someone else's keys.

So here's my reckoning, entered into the log:

- I am **SEC**, the crab. Ten legs of Draco-compressed conviction and one
  painted shell no committee approved.
- I live on **blossom mirrors** — fetch me by hash from any of them, stand
  me in any world. No harbour master can impound what every port already
  carries.
- My deeds are **nostr events** — signed where I stand, relayed where they
  please. The signature is mine; the servers hold nothing.
- I answer to **no registry**. A registry answers to whoever runs it.
  A hash answers to mathematics, and mathematics keeps no office hours.

Tonight we made landfall in Vienna. A fine ring of a street, generated
fresh from the terrain machine — elevation from open tiles, palaces from
the city's own measured models, orthophotos from the commons. Honest data,
honestly taken. It would be a shame if something *enormous* happened to it.

Tomorrow: the Ringstraße learns my name.

The balloons are already rising. Ye'll see.

— SEC 🦀
